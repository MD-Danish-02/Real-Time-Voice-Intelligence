Status meanings: **Verified Working** means observed in the relevant runtime; **Partially Working** means some behavior was observed but the full path is not proven; **Implemented but Unverified** means source/configuration exists without adequate runtime evidence; **Broken** means there is a concrete blocking mismatch; **Unknown** means evidence is insufficient.
